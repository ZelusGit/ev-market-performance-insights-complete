# Story sequence

The dashboard story navigator contains these five points, in order:

1. **EV Market Overview** — start with the selected-record count, recorded sales, average range, and average rating.
2. **Manufacturer Sales Performance** — compare the top manufacturers by recorded annual sales.
3. **Market Share Across Manufacturers** — show each leading manufacturer's portion of selected sales.
4. **Battery Technology Preferences** — compare the record mix for LFP, NCA, NMC, Sodium-Ion, and Solid-State technologies.
5. **Driving Range by Vehicle Category** — compare average range in kilometres across categories.

Use the dashboard filters before moving through the sequence to focus the story on a year, category, manufacturer, battery technology, or country.
